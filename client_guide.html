3. Complete Role Architecture & Workflow

Vows & Venues operates as a multi-sided luxury Indian wedding platform. Customers build celebrations and add multiple vendors to a single basket; vendors manage their storefronts and price packages; and the administration team monitors quality, handles KYC approvals, and oversees platform commissions.

4. Super Admin Operations Console (7 Enterprise Modules)

Logged in as admin@vowsandvenues.in, open the Admin Console to access all 7 operational suites:

1. Executive KPI Overview

  • Gross Merchandise Value (GMV): Live calculation of all booked celebrations across India.
  • Platform Revenue: Automated 10% take-rate earnings ledger.
  • Vendor Health: Real-time counts of total vendors, verified creators, and pending KYC submissions.

2. Vendor KYC & Verification Suite

  • Inspect Profiles: Review business name, contact, capacity, starting prices, and portfolios.
  • 1-Click Approval: Click "Verify" to grant the verified badge and publish the storefront.
  • Rejection Workflow: Ability to reject or request documentation updates with audit logs.

3. Master Bookings & Invoicing Ledger

  • Multi-Vendor Breakdown: Inspect which venues, caterers, decorators, and photographers are attached to each celebration.
  • Tax & Fee Transparency: Full financial calculation: Subtotal + 18% GST + Platform Fee - Coupon Discount = Total.
  • Advance Payment Tracking: Tracks 25% advance collected and 75% remaining balance.

4. Visual CMS Live Content Manager

  • No-Code Content Updates: Edit Hero headline, brand taglines, announcements, and trust metrics.
  • Instant Publishing: Updates save immediately to MongoDB Atlas without redeploying code.

5. Platform Finance & Vendor Settlement Ledger

  • Automated 10% Take-Rate: System automatically calculates 10% platform fee and 90% vendor payout.
  • Settlement Status: Track payout states (PENDING, PROCESSING, SETTLED) for each booked vendor.

6. Promo Codes & Coupon Engine

  • Active Codes: ROYAL2026 (₹15,000 off on ₹1,00,000+ orders) & SHUBH10 (10% off capped at ₹20,000).
  • Rule Builder: Create new codes with minimum order thresholds, discount caps, and expiry dates.

7. Luxury Media Library

  • Media Asset Manager: Upload, tag, and categorize high-resolution celebration photography.
  • Auto CDN: Optimized for mobile and desktop screens with instant caching.

5. Vendor Storefront & Portal Guide

1. 4-Step Onboarding Wizard

When a new wedding vendor registers on the platform, they are guided through a streamlined 4-step wizard:

  • 1 Business Info: Name, category (e.g. Venues, Catering, Decor), city, address, phone, and bio.
  • 2 Photos & Portfolio: Upload high-definition storefront cover image and celebration gallery.
  • 3 Categories & Pricing: Starting price per plate/day, capacity, and standard inclusions.
  • 4 Review & Publish: Direct submission for Admin KYC verification.

2. Tiered Package Creator

Vendors can create and customize tiered packages (e.g., Silver Package, Royal Heritage Bundle, Cinematic Drone Add-on) with individual pricing and deliverables.

3. Leads, Quotations & Booking Visibility

  • Inquiries: Receive inquiries from couples with specific event dates and guest counts.
  • Custom Quotes: Send customized price proposals directly through the portal.
  • Confirmed Bookings: View upcoming celebration dates and payout schedules.

6. Customer Experience & Event Builder Guide

1. Editorial Search & Multi-Filter Engine

  • Browse across 11 Indian wedding categories (Banquet Halls, Awadhi Catering, Mandaps, HD Makeup, Lehengas, Candid Cinema, DJ, Return Gifts, Garlands, E-Invites, Planners).
  • Filter by City (Lucknow, Delhi NCR, Jaipur, Udaipur, Mumbai, Bengaluru), Price Range, Rating (4.8+), and Verified Only.

2. All-in-One Event Builder Workspace

  • Unified Event Plan: Name the celebration (e.g. "Himanshu & Priya's Grand Wedding"), pick date, city, and guest count.
  • Multi-Vendor Cart: Add multiple independent vendors into one event (e.g. Royal Nawabi Palace + Shahi Dawat Catering + Gulmohar Luxury Decor + Drishti Wedding Cinema).
  • Live Budget Meter: Real-time visual tracking of committed budget vs. remaining balance.

3. Promotional Checkout & Invoicing

  • Coupon Codes: Enter ROYAL2026 or SHUBH10 to receive instant server-validated discounts.
  • 25% Advance Lock-in: Pay 25% today to reserve all vendors for the date; balance payable prior to the event.
  • Instant Tax Invoice: Downloads/displays official confirmation voucher with booking reference (VV-2026-XXXX).
